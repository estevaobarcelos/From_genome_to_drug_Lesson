STEP-BY-STEP INSTRUCTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------

Step 1 — Go to:
   blast.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov

Step 2 — Click:
   "Nucleotide BLAST (BLASTn)"

Step 3 — Paste the mystery sequence into the query box

Step 4 — Settings:
   Database:  Nucleotide collection (nr/nt)
   Organism:  leave empty
   Program:   Highly similar sequences (megablast)

Step 5 — Click BLAST and wait ~30 seconds


QUESTIONS TO ANSWER
--------------------------------------------------------------

Q1. What gene did BLAST identify?
    Write the gene name and the organism.

Q2. What is the E-value of the top hit?
    What does this E-value tell you about the match?

Q3. What is the % identity and % query coverage?

Q4. On which chromosome and at which position
    does this gene reside?

Q5. Scroll down the hit list.
    Can you find the same gene in other species?
    Which species appear, and what are their % identities?

Q6. Look at the alignment shown by BLAST.
    Can you identify any mismatches or gaps?
    What could these represent biologically?

Q7. Based on what you find — why do you think
    this gene might be important in immunology
    and cancer therapy?
